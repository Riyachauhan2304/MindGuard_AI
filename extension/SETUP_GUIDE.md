# MindGuard - Setup & Getting Started Guide

## 🚀 Quick Start (2 Minutes)

MindGuard works **instantly** with no login required. Just install and start blocking!

### Step 1: Install the Extension

1. Open your browser and go to `chrome://extensions/`
2. Enable **Developer mode** (toggle in top-right)
3. Click **Load unpacked**
4. Navigate to the `/extension/` folder
5. Click **Select folder**

### Step 2: Open MindGuard

- Click the **MindGuard icon** in your toolbar
- You'll see your dashboard with a "Guest User" profile
- **That's it!** You're ready to go

### Step 3: Add Your First Rule

**Option A: Quick Add (Recommended)**
1. Click **⚙️ Settings** in the popup
2. Go to the **Rules** tab
3. Click on any popular site (YouTube, Instagram, etc.)
4. Site is now blocked!

**Option B: Custom Domain**
1. Click **⚙️ Settings**
2. Go to **Rules** tab
3. Enter a domain (e.g., `twitter.com`)
4. Select **Block** action
5. Click **Add Rule**

### Step 4: Test It

1. Try visiting a blocked site
2. See the **blocking page** with challenges
3. Complete any challenge (Breathing, Quiz, Typing, Riddle) to unlock for 5 minutes
4. Get back to focused work!

## ⚙️ Settings & Configuration

Click **⚙️ Settings** to access the full options page with 4 tabs:

### Tab 1: Rules
**Manage your blocklist**

- **Add New Rule**: Enter domain name and select block/allow action
- **Your Rules**: View all current rules, delete unwanted ones
- **Quick Add**: One-click blocking for popular sites
  - YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter
  - Reddit, TikTok, Netflix, Twitch
  - Pinterest, LinkedIn, Snapchat, Discord

### Tab 2: Settings
**Customize your experience**

- **⏰ Focus Hours**
  - Start Time: When blocking should begin (default: 09:00)
  - End Time: When blocking should stop (default: 17:00)

- **🎯 Blocking Preferences**
  - **Strict Mode**: Block 24/7, even outside focus hours
  - **Weekend Mode**: Softer rules on weekends
  - **Notifications**: Alerts when sites are blocked

- **🎨 Appearance**
  - **Theme**: Light theme (more options coming)

### Tab 3: Analytics
**Track your progress**

- **Overall Stats**: Total blocked, completed challenges, focus score
- **Most Blocked Sites**: Top distracting sites for you
- **Productivity Insights**: Your focus patterns

### Tab 4: Profile
**Account & preferences**

- **Profile Info**: Your default "Guest User" account
- **Location & Language**: Country and language preferences
- **Reset Data**: Clear all activity logs and rules (irreversible)

## 🎮 Challenge System

When you try to access a blocked site, you can unlock it temporarily by completing a challenge:

### 🧘 Breathing Exercise
- Follow on-screen breathing prompts
- Helps calm your mind and refocus
- Duration: ~2 minutes

### 🧠 Quiz
- Answer quick questions
- Tests your general knowledge
- Duration: ~1-2 minutes

### ⌨️ Typing Test
- Type a provided passage
- Improves focus and typing skills
- Duration: ~2-3 minutes

### 🔮 Riddle
- Solve a logic puzzle
- Activates problem-solving skills
- Duration: ~1-2 minutes

**After completing any challenge:**
- Site is unlocked for 5 minutes
- Timer starts counting down
- You can access the site during this window

## 🔒 Privacy & Data

### All Data is Local
- Nothing is sent to servers
- No cloud storage
- No data collection
- Completely private

### What Data is Stored?
- Your blocklist (domains)
- Activity logs (which sites, when)
- Stats (focus score, counts)
- Settings (preferences)

### Data Storage Location
- Chrome's local storage on **your device only**
- Persists even after browser restart
- Stored in your user profile folder

### Clearing Your Data
1. Go to **Settings** → **Profile** tab
2. Click **🗑️ Reset All Data**
3. Confirm the action
4. All data is immediately deleted

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### Blocking Isn't Working

**Check these:**
1. Is the toggle **enabled** in the popup? (should be green)
2. Is the domain in your rules list?
3. Hard refresh the page: `Ctrl+Shift+R` (or `Cmd+Shift+R` on Mac)
4. Check if "Strict Mode" is enabled in settings

**If still blocked:**
- Reload the extension: Go to `chrome://extensions/` → Click refresh on MindGuard
- Try a different blocked site to test
- Check your internet connection

### Settings Not Saving

**Try:**
1. Make sure you clicked the **Save** button
2. Check browser console for errors: `Ctrl+Shift+J`
3. Verify storage quota isn't exceeded
4. Try closing and reopening the extension

### Popup Won't Open

**Solutions:**
1. Verify extension is enabled in `chrome://extensions/`
2. Unpin and repin the extension
3. Reload the extension (click refresh icon)
4. Restart your browser

### Stats/Activity Not Updating

1. Reload the extension
2. Refresh the page you're on
3. Close and reopen the popup
4. Check if "Blocking" toggle is enabled

## 💡 Tips & Best Practices

### For Maximum Productivity

1. **Start small**: Add 3-5 sites you struggle with most
2. **Use Strict Mode**: For complete focus during work hours
3. **Review Weekly**: Check analytics to see patterns
4. **Adjust as needed**: Remove rules that aren't helping

### For Using Challenges

1. **Pick challenges that work for you**: Some prefer breathing, others like quizzes
2. **Use delays strategically**: Completing challenges gives your brain a break
3. **Don't build habits**: Challenges should be occasional, not routine

### For Better Focus

1. **Set realistic focus hours**: 9-5 for office work, adjust for remote
2. **Use Weekend Mode**: Slightly relax on weekends to prevent burnout
3. **Monitor your stats**: High blocked counts show when you struggle most
4. **Celebrate progress**: Look at your focus score and time saved!

## 🔄 Resetting vs. Disabling

### Disable Blocking Temporarily
- Click the **toggle switch** in popup
- Blocking pauses immediately
- All settings preserved
- Re-enable whenever ready

### Reset All Data
- **Profile** tab → **Reset All Data**
- Clears activity logs, stats, and rules
- Keeps settings and profile
- Good for fresh start

### Uninstall Extension
- Go to `chrome://extensions/`
- Click **Remove** on MindGuard card
- All data is permanently deleted
- Can reinstall anytime

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**
A: No! The extension works instantly with a default "Guest User" profile.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes! All data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is shared or uploaded.

**Q: Can I sync across devices?**
A: Not yet. Each device maintains its own separate blocklist.

**Q: What if I need to access a blocked site in an emergency?**
A: Toggle off blocking in the popup, visit the site, then re-enable.

**Q: Can I modify the blocklist during work?**
A: Yes, but you need to complete a challenge first to access blocked sites.

**Q: How long does an unlock last?**
A: 5 minutes after completing a challenge.

**Q: Can I back up my rules?**
A: Manually, by taking a screenshot of your rules list. Automatic export coming soon.

**Q: Does this work in Incognito mode?**
A: Limited support. Rules may not persist in Incognito on some browsers.
